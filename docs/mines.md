# Mining Worlds

Two permanent mining worlds with server-controlled ore generation:

| Mine | Ores | PvP |
|---|---|---|
| **Stonewake** | Coal, Iron, Redstone | KOTH zone only |
| **Bloodvein** | Gold, Lapis, Diamond, Emerald, Netherite Block | Everywhere |

Vertex does not create the worlds. Import them however you like (Multiverse
or otherwise) — Vertex only controls what happens inside the region you
select.

## Placing a mine

The two mines ship fully configured except for *where* they are. You define
that in-game rather than by typing coordinates, exactly like `/koth create`:

```
/mines wand stonewake     gives the blaze rod and starts a selection
left-click                first corner
right-click               second corner
sneak + click air         saves it
```

Saving writes the **world and both corners into `mines.yml` for you** — the
world is taken from where you were standing, so the config can never name a
world your selection does not match. Until a mine has been placed this way
its `world` stays blank and it is simply inactive.

`/mines list` shows which are placed. `/mines cancel` abandons a selection.

## Why player-placed ore is impossible

Placing blocks inside a mine region is denied outright, and players may only
break blocks the mine generates. Together those two rules mean **anything
inside the region was generated by the mine** — so "is this ore legitimate?"
needs no per-block tracking of half a million positions, and the usual fake
ore exploits (placing your own, hauling blocks in, place/break farming)
aren't reachable at all.

Structure, walls, floor, and decoration are protected automatically by the
same rule, without having to be listed anywhere.

## Drops

Vertex owns the drop completely inside a mine:

- **Silk Touch does nothing** — it cannot yield the ore block itself.
- **Fortune does nothing** — per the finalized spec, it has no effect on
  Vertex-generated ore.
- Only the configured base drop and the **Ore Drop** booster decide the
  amount. See [Boosters](boosters.md).

A bonus above 100% becomes guaranteed extra drops plus a chance at one more,
so +150% is always at least 2.5x and averages exactly that rather than
rounding the fraction away.

Base blocks (Stone, Deepslate) regenerate but pay nothing.

## Ore tables

Weights are relative and do **not** have to total 100 — editing one ore
never means rebalancing the rest. Shipped defaults:

**Stonewake** — 90% base, 4.5% coal, 3.5% iron, 2% redstone (10% ore).

**Bloodvein** — 96.195% base, 1.5% gold, 1% lapis, 0.8% diamond, 0.5%
emerald, **0.005% Netherite Block** (roughly one in twenty thousand).

## Regeneration

A mined block is cleared to a base block immediately and queued to
regenerate after `regen-delay-seconds` (30 by default), rerolling the ore
table when it does.

A 700×700 floor is 490,000 positions per layer, so regeneration is driven
entirely by **what was actually mined**: one shared worker drains a
timestamp queue in bounded batches (100 per pass, every 5 ticks by default).
The region is never scanned, and there is never a scheduled task per block.

If more locations come due than a pass allows, the oldest go first and the
rest stay queued — a busy queue never drops a location, since that block
would otherwise stay mined out forever. Before regenerating, the location is
re-checked: if the region moved or the area was rebuilt while it waited, it
is skipped rather than pasted into someone's structure.
